# brag-plan — "twofold" Blender product film

**What it is:** a 16.4s vertical 3D product spot for *twofold* (a daily antioxidant supplement), built and rendered in Blender.
**Who it's for:** brands and art directors scrolling for a 3D / motion designer, plus the Blender crowd.
**Sets it apart:** the brand idea is literally in the render: two-tone green/white capsules, a "two"/"fold" wordmark, a two-part tin.
**Visual hook:** a full-frame macro of capsules, with the wordmark snapping in on top.
**Angle:** let the render do the talking. Keep the original edit intact, frame it with the brand's own type, give it a mixed soundtrack, and end on a clean card.
**Tone:** polished: restrained type, long holds, soft fades.
**Format:** vertical 1080×1920, 30fps, 20.0s.
**Identity:** tin olive #8f9a4c · capsule green #3a9a35 · warm grey #99938e · cream #ece8e1 · ink #23211e. Font: Outfit (closest to the geometric wordmark).
**Share caption:** see share-copy.txt.
